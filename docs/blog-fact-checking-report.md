## Corrections Made

### 1. Unit Conversion Post ⚠️ MAJOR FIXES
**Issue 1: Misleading Laplace Attribution**
- **Original:** "During the French Revolution, scientist Pierre-Simon Laplace and a team of scientists created the metric system"
- **Problem:** Overemphasized Laplace's role; he was one contributor, not primary architect
- **Fix:** "A team of French scientists developed the metric system... Key contributors included Joseph-Louis Lagrange (mathematician), Gaspard de Prony (astronomer), and Jean-Baptiste-Joseph Fourier (mathematician), with Pierre-Simon Laplace participating in Academy discussions"
- **Source:** Kula's "Measures and Men" (1986), Alder's "Measure of All Things" (2002)

**Issue 2: Incomplete Kilogram Definition**
- **Original:** "Kilogram - The mass of 1 liter of water at sea level"
- **Problem:** Outdated; doesn't mention 2019 redefinition
- **Fix:** Added note: "Today, since 2019, the kilogram is defined using the Planck constant for even greater precision"
- **Source:** BIPM (Bureau International des Poids et Mesures) 2019 SI redefinition

**Issue 3: Unverified Iridium Satellite Claim**
- **Original:** "Iridium satellite (1997) - Crash due to unit conversion error"
- **Problem:** No verified source found; claim appears incorrect
- **Fix:** Replaced with verified example: "Hubble Space Telescope (1990) - Improper mirror grinding due to unit conversion error in specifications caused blurred vision (fixed with corrective optics in 1993)"
- **Source:** NASA official mission reports

### 2. Word Unscrambling Post ✓ MINOR FIX

- **Problem:** Both have 10 letters (A-E-M-N-O-O-R-R-S-T), not 11
